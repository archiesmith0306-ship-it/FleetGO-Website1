2Billing

  • Billed monthly, in arrears, by the number of active days per truck.
  • Active day: any calendar day on which the truck has a trip or recorded movement in Samsara or Motive.
  • Charge per truck = daily rate × active days in the month (capped at the monthly rate).
  • Monthly minimum: equal to 10 trucks at the selected rate. Applies if the active-day charges come to less.
  • Invoice due [Net 10 / Net 30]. A detailed active-day report is attached to each invoice.

3What's included

  • Fleet Management: PM Monitoring (preventative maintenance scheduling), PTI Monitoring (pre-trip inspection compliance), Cases (maintenance case tracking end to end), Follow-ups (on-time case closure), work orders issued at case close, weekly maintenance expense statement, 24/7 support and a dedicated account manager.
  • RoadWatch Safety Monitoring: live monitoring of safety events and telematics alerts, immediate driver calls when an alert fires, escalation to the company and a safety dashboard with response reporting.
  • Optional add-ons (quoted separately): Trailer Tracking & Control — $30/trailer/mo (1–25), $20 (26+) · Supply Chain Procurement — 50% of the first-time supplier fee · Fuel Saving Platform — 25% of verified monthly savings.

4Terms

  • Term: 6 months from the start date, with cancellation at any time.
  • Cancellation: 30 days' written notice.
  • Rates: fixed for the term. Changes in fleet size are billed by the active-day method.
  • Setup: Samsara or Motive admin access for FleetGO staff is required before the start date.
  • Not included: repair and parts costs, towing, and third-party fees.

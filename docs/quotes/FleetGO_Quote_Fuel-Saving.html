2How savings are measured

  • Every recommended fuel stop is logged with its route, station and price, and sent to the driver.
  • A fill-up counts only when it is matched to a FleetGO recommendation (status "Followed"). Fill-ups that did not follow a recommendation are excluded.
  • Saving per fill-up = (median price at comparable stops on the corridor − price actually paid) × gallons purchased.
  • Prices are evaluated net of state fuel tax, with your fuel-card discounts applied where the stop is on-network.
  • The full proven-savings ledger (route log, matched fill-ups, savings per fill-up) is available in the platform and exportable to CSV.

3Billing

  • Billed monthly, in arrears, at 25% of the verified savings for that month.
  • No verified savings, no fee for that month.
  • The proven-savings ledger for the month is attached to each invoice.
  • Invoice due [Net 10 / Net 30].

4What's included

  • Fuel route planning: start-to-destination corridor with every stop priced at your actual card rate, and truck routing that avoids tolls, tunnels, dirt roads and ferries.
  • Never-fuel-here map: state-by-state fuel cost, route risk and tax rate, flagging the most expensive states to skip.
  • Live fleet view: fuel level and location for every unit with low-fuel alerts, via your Samsara or Motive account.
  • Price trends: average diesel by chain with 14 days of history and the biggest state-level movers.
  • 24/7 monitoring of recommended stops and fill-up matching.

5Terms

  • Term: 6 months from the start date, with cancellation at any time.
  • Cancellation: 30 days' written notice.
  • Rate: the 25% fee is fixed for the term.
  • Setup: Samsara or Motive admin access and fuel-card program details are required before the start date.
  • Not included: the cost of fuel itself, fuel-card fees, and third-party fees.
